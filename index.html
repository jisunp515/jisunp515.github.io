"Designing First-Order Methods with Provable Guarantees via a Data-Driven Approach"

I am a postdoctoral research associate at the Department of Operations Research and Financial Engineering at Princeton University, working with Prof. Bartolomeo Stellato.

My research interest lies in mathematical optimization and theoretical aspects of machine learning. My recent work focuses on data-driven analysis and design of first-order methods, providing performance guarantees for optimization algorithms via distributionally robust optimization and exact verification. I also develop FOMTools, an open-source package for verifying first-order methods and generating solver code for them with AI assistance, and work on accelerating general-purpose first-order solvers.

I obtained Ph.D. from the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Seoul National University (SNU), where I studied classical fixed-point iterations from both theoretical and practical perspectives. My advisor was Prof. Ernest K. Ryu.
